About Us

Your trusted partner in regulatory compliance.

SIGMA is a local company with deep experience in safety and regulatory compliance across the transportation and mechanical & electrical industries. We offer professional services specializing in internal audits of ICOP Safety documents, training, and safety-related courses.

Our commitment is to help organizations meet the requirements of authorities such as APAD (Land Public Transport Agency) and comply with ICOP Safety standards — ensuring your operations are safe, compliant, and audit-ready.
